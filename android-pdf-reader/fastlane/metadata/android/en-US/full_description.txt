ECZ PDF reader opens and reads PDFs, no frills.

• Light: about 50 KB, opens instantly.
• No ads and no tracking. The app requests no permissions and has no Internet access.
• Continuous scrolling, pinch or double-tap zoom, fast-scroll bar showing the page number.
• Opens from the app button or from "Open with" in file managers, browsers and email.
• Recent files with thumbnails: resume from the last page you read.
• Text search with highlighted results (on Android 15, or older versions with recent system updates).
• Go to page, night mode, share the file.
• Light and dark theme, in Italian and English.

Limits: the app doesn't open password-protected PDFs, doesn't fill in forms and can't search the text of scanned PDFs (which contain only images).
